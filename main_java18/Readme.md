#Linked List

Definition:-
A Linked list is a doubly-Linked list implementation of the list and Deque interfaces in java.

Advantages:- 
. Efficient insertions and deletions at the beginining or end.
. Useful when Frequent modifications occur.

Disadvantage:-
. Slower random access compared to arraylist.

Common Methods:-
. add(),
  addFirst(),
  addLast().

. remove(),
  removeFirst(),
  removeLast().

. get(),
  getFirst(),
  getLast(),
  set().

. size(),
  contains(),
  clear().    

Key Point:-
LinkedList is optimal for scenarias that require many insertions and deletions.
